Privacy Policy

TV Mode for Power BI

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

TV Mode for Power BI is a Chrome browser extension that helps users display Power BI reports on TV screens with automatic rotation and fullscreen mode. This extension is an independent third-party tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft Corporation.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

This extension does not:

  • Collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers
  • Track browsing activity or history
  • Access the content of your Power BI reports
  • Share any data with third parties

3. Data Stored Locally

The extension stores the following data only on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API:

DataPurpose
Power BI page URLs you addTo remember your slideshow pages between sessions
Rotation interval (seconds)To save your preferred timing setting
Data refresh interval (minutes)To save your preferred refresh setting

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by removing the extension or clearing Chrome's extension storage.

4. Permissions Explained

PermissionWhy it is needed
tabsTo read the URL and title of the active Power BI tab when you click "Add current tab"
windowsTo open and manage the fullscreen TV presentation window
storageTo save your slideshow configuration locally on your device
Host: app.powerbi.comTo inject CSS that hides the Power BI interface elements (filter pane, navigation bar) on Power BI pages
